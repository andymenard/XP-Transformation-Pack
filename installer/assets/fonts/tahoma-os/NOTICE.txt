Tahoma OS — locally renamed Microsoft Tahoma

The regular and bold faces are derived from the supplied Microsoft Tahoma
fonts by changing family/face naming metadata and required checksums. Glyph
outlines, metrics, hinting programs and original copyright/trademark/license
records remain unchanged. Microsoft retains the original font copyrights.
These font assets are separate from the project's GPL-licensed code.
See PROVENANCE.json for the source and derived-file SHA-256 hashes.
