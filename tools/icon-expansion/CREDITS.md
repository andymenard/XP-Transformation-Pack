The user's original Microsoft icons remain separately installed in the primary
XPlasma theme, with their original rights unchanged.

Specialized action/diagram/math glyphs without an XP counterpart use modified
KDE Breeze SVG geometry with XP blue and bevel treatment. KDE artwork copyright:
Uri Herrera and the KDE Breeze contributors. These derivatives retain the
original artwork licensing; they are not CC0 or original Microsoft icons.
Their unmodified SVG source is bundled in KDE-Breeze-SVG; see COPYING-KDE-ICONS
and each ALIAS-PROVENANCE.json entry. KDE source project:
https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons

The submenu triangles, window-control silhouettes and expand/collapse controls
are original XPlasma GPL-3.0-or-later artwork. Symbolic variants retain a clear
alpha silhouette because KDE may recolor them as masks. Full-color XP browser
Back/Forward icons remain separate from those control glyphs.
